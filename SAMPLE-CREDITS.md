Source pages and license labels checked on 16 October 2026.

Preparation: Heavenly Choir is a 14-second segment starting at 4 seconds from the creator's WAV download. Cloud Drift is a 16-second segment starting at 10 seconds from Freesound's publicly served high-quality MP3 preview of the licensed recording. Both were resampled to 32 kHz stereo, encoded as 16-bit FLAC and retain their recorded pitch. Abide normalizes playback and crossfades the loop seam. There is no automatic major/minor conversion: transposing a recorded chord preserves its chord quality.

## Import your own drone sounds

The native app and VST3 also accept WAV, MP3, FLAC and AIFF files through **Import Drones**. Imported sounds are copied into a private local sample cache; they are not uploaded, sent to GitHub or redistributed with Abide.

Useful places to find additional recordings:

- Pixabay worship pads: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/worship%20pads/
- Freesound: https://freesound.org/
- OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/

Check the individual recording's license before using or redistributing it. Pixabay's standard content license restricts redistribution of substantially unchanged recordings; its files are therefore not bundled with this release.
