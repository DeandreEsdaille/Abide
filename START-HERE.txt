ABIDE 0.2 — Gimme That Juice

NEW DRONE SOUNDS
Use DRONE SOUND to choose Heavenly Choir, Cloud Drift, or the original synthesizer.
The two factory recordings are included, so you do not need to download anything.
Click C through B or send MIDI to play. Switch sounds while a drone is playing.
Reverb, Delay and the other controls work on every sound.

YOUR OWN RECORDINGS
1. Download a sound you are licensed to use (WAV, MP3, FLAC or AIFF).
2. Click IMPORT DRONES and choose one or several files (8 imported slots).
3. Select its name in DRONE SOUND.
4. Set SAMPLE ROOT to the recording's original key, then choose your playback key.
Names beginning with C-, Db-, F#-, etc. provide an initial root guess.
Abide remembers the local sample references in your saved project/app state.

Imports are cached locally, never uploaded. Abide uses up to 60 seconds of a
recording, starting 5 seconds in for files longer than 90 seconds. Pitch changes
also change the recording's playback speed. Chords retain major/minor quality.

Fade Out releases the drone; Panic stops sound and clears effects immediately.

Installers include standalone and VST3 versions. Close your DAW before updating,
then rescan plugins if needed. The installers are unsigned prerelease builds.

Sample creator credits and links are in SAMPLE-CREDITS.md.
Preview.html is the older browser synthesizer preview; sample importing and
factory recordings in version 0.2 are native app/VST3 features.
